How to Use

Tap anywhere to start or pause the timer.

Long press to reset.

Open Settings (gear icon) to customize focus and break durations.

About the Colors

During a focus session, the background cycles through 5 color harmony modes — Complementary, Split Complementary, Triadic, Tetradic, and Analogous — shifting base hues every 25 seconds.
