Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 25, 2026

Overview

Color Grid Pomodoro is a focus timer app that respects your privacy. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

Data Storage

All data (timer settings and daily session count) is stored locally on your device using standard system preferences. No data leaves your device.

Analytics & Tracking

This app does not use any analytics services, advertising frameworks, or tracking technologies.

Third-Party Services

This app does not integrate any third-party services or SDKs.

Notifications

The app may request permission to send local notifications to alert you when a focus or break session ends. This is entirely optional and processed on-device.

Children's Privacy

This app does not knowingly collect any information from children.

Changes

If this policy is updated, the changes will be posted on this page.

Contact

If you have questions, please open an issue on the GitHub repository.
